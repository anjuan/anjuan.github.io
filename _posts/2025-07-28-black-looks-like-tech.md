## Conclusion

The "pipeline problem" narrative is like blaming your deployment issues on the CI/CD pipeline while ignoring your application architecture. The real issues - and solutions - are more complex and systemic.

Success requires:
- Comprehensive approach
- Sustained commitment
- Real resources
- Measurable accountability

Remember: The goal isn't just to get more Black people into tech - it's to create an industry where Black technologists can thrive, lead, and transform the future of technology.

The question isn't "Why aren't there more Black people in tech?"

The question is: "What are we doing to transform tech into an industry where Black talent can flourish?"
