Building a speaking career is like developing a complex application:
- Start with a solid foundation

- Gather user feedback

Remember: Every speaker you admire started exactly where you are. The key is to:
- Start small
- Practice consistently
- Learn continuously
- Stay authentic

Your first talk might feel like a beta release, but with time, testing, and refinement, you can build a speaking career that scales globally.
