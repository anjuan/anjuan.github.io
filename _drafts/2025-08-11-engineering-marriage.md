## Conclusion

After twenty-five years, I've learned that marriage, like software:
- Requires continuous maintenance
- Benefits from regular updates
- Needs careful error handling
- Improves with documentation

The key differences? 
- You can't ctrl+z conflicts
- Production is always live
- The stakes are much higher
- The rewards are infinitely greater

Remember: Like any complex system, a marriage requires constant attention, regular maintenance, and ongoing commitment to improvement. But unlike software, the returns on this investment - love, growth, shared joy - are truly priceless.

*To my wife: Thank you for twenty-five years of pair programming in life. Here's to our next release cycle together.*
